by Emil Kowalski, originally published in `emilkowalski/skills`.

The original copyright and MIT license are preserved in `LICENSE` and `source-baseline/LICENSE`.
The v1 SpaceAge expansion is preserved in `source-baseline/spaceage-v1-SKILL.md`.

The derivative name **SpaceAge Apple-Immersive Web Design** describes an Apple-inspired design
methodology. It is not an Apple product, is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple, and does
not reproduce Apple's proprietary visual assets or private rendering systems.

"Liquid Glass-inspired" refers to a web approximation of functional translucent material built
from public browser capabilities (`backdrop-filter`, SVG displacement maps, layered gradients).
It does not claim pixel-identical or system-native Liquid Glass rendering.

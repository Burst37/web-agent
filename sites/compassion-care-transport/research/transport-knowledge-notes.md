## Evidence and business boundaries

Provider descriptions informed mobility categories, access planning, appointment arrival, recurring dialysis/therapy, discharge readiness and facility handoffs. Provider-specific prices, hours, credentials, insurance contracts, clinical staff, lifting, oxygen, stretcher services and vehicle capacities were not adopted as Compassion Care claims. Texas HHSC informs program navigation only. Medicare.gov describes ambulance benefits, not a guarantee for a routine van ride.

### Ambulatory transportation

Ambulatory describes the passenger’s mobility, rather than the reason for the appointment. Planning focuses on getting to the vehicle, boarding, storing walking aids and reaching the destination entrance. Tell us about the help you actually need so the team can check that the trip is suitable.

References: dream, local.

### Wheelchair transportation

Wheelchair transportation planning covers more than vehicle space. The team needs to review the chair, boarding access and how the wheelchair and passenger will be secured. A power chair, scooter, wider chair or difficult entrance needs advance discussion; suitability is confirmed for the individual trip.

References: dream, local.

### Hospital discharges

Discharge transport depends on when the patient is actually ready to leave. Coordinate the pickup entrance, ready time, mobility needs and receiving contact with the discharge planner or nurse. If travel needs change, tell both teams before the ride; transportation does not replace discharge instructions or clinical care.

References: mts, local.

### Doctor appointments

A successful appointment trip starts with the clinic’s required arrival time, which may be earlier than the appointment itself. Provide the correct building and entrance, boarding needs and whether someone will accompany the passenger. Discuss the return separately, especially when the length of the appointment is uncertain.

References: dream, mts.

### Dialysis transportation

Dialysis transportation planning centers on a dependable appointment pattern and clear return arrangements. Share treatment days, required arrival and the center’s expected finish time. Tell the team about mobility or assistance needs for both legs, and report schedule changes promptly. Recurring rides still require confirmation of availability.

References: mts, dream.

### Rehabilitation appointments

Recovery needs can change between appointments. Tell the team what walking aids or wheelchair arrangements are needed now, rather than relying on a previous trip’s details. Include the therapy location, arrival time and return estimate. Transportation helps you reach treatment; exercise instructions and clinical decisions remain with the treating professionals.

References: mts, local.

### Assisted living and nursing facility transportation

Facility transportation connects the resident, the sending facility and the receiving destination. Planning should identify who will have the resident ready, where the driver should meet them, and who will receive them after the trip. Confirm mobility needs, access arrangements and the return contact before transportation is accepted.

References: mts, local.
